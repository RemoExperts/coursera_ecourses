Where Human Data Work Fits in Robotics

A robot sees the world, decides what to do, and moves. Between those three steps is a huge amount of human-created data. This module shows where that data comes from — and where your work fits.

⏱ About 20–25 minutes🦾 6 interactive drills✓ 6 knowledge checks

Before you start — use the page, don't skim it

Interactive Module 1 · production data literacy, not robotics engineering
  1. Move things. Flip cards, drive the arm, classify projects and inspect the lifecycle.
  2. Watch what changes. Every interaction is designed to make one idea visible, not to decorate the page.
  3. Stay at the project level. Joint anatomy, TCP, coordinate frames and detailed robot mechanics belong to the next module.
  4. Your goal: look at a new robotics project and explain its input, human task, output and downstream use.
Use one question throughout: What goes in → what does the human do → what comes out → who uses it next?
